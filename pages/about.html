Businesses don’t need more AI demos. They need AI that works on Monday morning.

I’m Arunprakash — a Senior GenAI Engineer with 5+ years in software engineering and 3+ years shipping applied Generative AI. My focus is the unglamorous part where value actually lives: taking agentic AI, RAG pipelines, and enterprise integrations from a promising prototype to a governed, observable, production system your teams rely on every day.

I own solutions end to end — architecture, development, CI/CD, observability, and 24×7 production support — and I work closely with product, data, and platform stakeholders to turn business requirements into systems with measurable outcomes. In 2025 that approach won first place at an enterprise innovation hackathon, for a production-ready agentic AI system judged on business impact and execution quality.
